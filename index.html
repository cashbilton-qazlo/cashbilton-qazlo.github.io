Mathematical Foundations: GPU Fluid Dynamics

Navier-Stokes Equations

This simulator solves the incompressible, Eulerian Navier-Stokes equations for fluid motion. The fluid is treated as a continuous field rather than discrete particles.

Conservation of Momentum
∂u/∂t + (u · ∇)u = -(1/ρ)∇p + ν∇²u + f
Conservation of Mass (Incompressibility)
∇ · u = 0
Chorin's Projection Method

To solve these equations in real-time, we use Operator Splitting (Fractional Steps) computed entirely on the GPU:

  • Advection: Calculates how the fluid's own velocity moves itself and properties like dye or density using a Semi-Lagrangian integration scheme.
  • Vorticity Confinement: Re-injects microscopic rotational energy (curl) lost due to the numerical dissipation of the grid to simulate realistic turbulence.
  • Pressure Poisson: Uses Jacobi iterations to solve the elliptical equation ∇²p = ρ/Δt ∇ · u*, ensuring the fluid remains volume-conserving (incompressible) when flowing around obstacles.

Mathematical Foundations: Isentropic Nozzle Flow

De Laval Dynamics

The liquid rocket sizer utilizes 1-Dimensional Ideal Isentropic Flow relations. It assumes the combustion gases behave as an ideal gas expanding adiabatically through a convergent-divergent (De Laval) nozzle.

The flow accelerates to Mach 1 (sonic velocity) exactly at the nozzle throat, and continues accelerating to supersonic velocities in the divergent section as thermal energy is converted into directed kinetic energy.

Throat Area (A_t)

Determined by choking conditions (Mach 1) for the requested mass flow and chamber pressure.

A_t = (ṁ / P_c) * √(R·T_c / γ) * ((γ+1)/2)^((γ+1)/(2(γ-1)))
Expansion Ratio (ε)

The ratio of the exit area to the throat area, dictating how perfectly the exhaust expands to match ambient pressure.

ε = A_e / A_t = 1/M_e * [(2 + (γ-1)M_e²) / (γ+1)]^((γ+1)/(2(γ-1)))
Specific Impulse (I_sp)

Measures engine efficiency; defined as thrust per unit weight flow rate of propellant.

I_sp = (C_f * c*) / g_0 | F = ṁ * v_e + (p_e - p_a)A_e

Where C_f is the Thrust Coefficient and c* is the Characteristic Velocity.
